# Athlete research notes

These notes are the source for `data/athletes.js`. At 16–32 px tall a face carries no identity, so
each athlete is recognized by **traits**: skin tone, hair, headwear, eyewear, kit colors and
pattern, build, and one signature detail.

The traits come from reference photos that are kept outside this repo; no photo is ever used in
the game. Kit colors were checked against 2026-season coverage in September 2026, and the kit
choices below were confirmed by the project owner on 2026-09-22. Anything marked **estimate** is
unconfirmed.

Heights are from ProCyclingStats / Wikipedia.

| Remco Evenepoel | 171 cm, compact | fair skin, dark-brown hair, gold mirrored glasses | Red Bull-BORA-hansgrohe: white with bright-blue sleeves | rainbow cuffs (time-trial world champion) |
| Wout van Aert | 190 cm, tallest and broad | fair skin, dark-brown slicked-back hair | Visma-Lease a Bike: yellow, fading to black at the bottom | the tallest rider in the field |
| Tom Pidcock | 170 cm, smallest | fair skin, light-brown hair | Pinarello-Q36.5: navy with gold accents (his reference photo shows his old INEOS kit) | the most compact body on the bike |

| Jonas Vingegaard | 175 cm, very slim | fair skin, light-brown hair, yellow helmet | Visma-Lease a Bike (same team as van Aert) | slimmer and shorter than van Aert; his teammate's opposite build is what tells them apart |

Pogačar's reference photo shows the Tour de France yellow jersey. In-game he wears UAE white so
that he doesn't blend into the two yellow Visma riders.
